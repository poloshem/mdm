What we do

Our Ministries

Eight ways we serve communities and share the Gospel.

1. Evangelism

We share the Gospel of Jesus Christ through preaching, community outreach, crusades, church visits, and personal evangelism.

Our desire is to reach people with the message of salvation and encourage them to develop a relationship with God.

2. Prayer Ministry

Prayer is central to our ministry.

We organize prayer gatherings, home prayer visits, church prayer activities, and online prayer encouragement. We welcome people who need prayer and spiritual encouragement.

3. Community Outreach

We believe Christian service should reach beyond the church.

Where resources permit, we seek to support vulnerable individuals and families through practical assistance, encouragement, and community activities.

4. Youth Ministry

We encourage young people to grow spiritually, develop positive values, discover their gifts, and participate in Christian fellowship and service.

5. Music Ministry

Music is an important part of Christian worship and evangelism.

We encourage young people and other believers to use their musical gifts to worship God and reach others with the Gospel.

6. Online Evangelism

The internet allows the Gospel to reach people far beyond our local community.

Through Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and other online platforms, Apostle Peter Otieno Angir shares preaching, Bible verses, prayer messages, testimonies, and Christian encouragement.

7. Pastoral Care & Spiritual Encouragement

We provide spiritual encouragement and pastoral support to people who need someone to listen, pray with them, and encourage them in their walk with God.

8. Outreach to People Seeking a New Beginning

We have a heart for people facing difficult social and economic circumstances.

Our desire is to encourage people toward a new beginning, dignity, faith, and a positive future.

Miracle and Deliverance Ministry

A Christian ministry based in Homa Bay County, Kenya, founded by Apostle Peter Otieno Angir. We share the Gospel, encourage people in their faith, and serve vulnerable communities through prayer, outreach, and practical assistance.
